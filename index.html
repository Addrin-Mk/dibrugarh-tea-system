The Journey of Dibrugarh Tea: From Mist to Cup

Scroll through the four essential milestones of tea transformation in Upper Assam, from morning plucking on estate slopes to electronic trading at GTAC.

Stage 1 of 4 • Field Plucking

Dawn Harvest: Two Leaves and a Bud

At first light across Dibrugarh's 37,254 hectares, over 127,000 workers begin the fine plucking ritual, selecting only the tender terminal bud and two adjoining leaves.

Step 01 • Plantation Harvest

Hand-Plucking at Dawn

Women pluckers with bamboo woven baskets (khorahi) meticulously harvest fresh green shoots before the mid-day sun evaporates delicate essential aromatic oils.

Step 02 • Factory Processing

Withering & CTC / Orthodox Rolling

Green leaves lose 35% moisture in aeration troughs before passing through CTC (Crush, Tear, Curl) rollers for strong milk teas or gentle orthodox roller tables for tippy whole leaves.

Step 03 • Aroma Development

Oxidation & Fluid-Bed Firing

Enzymatic fermentation turns leaves from green to copper brown, developing Dibrugarh’s trademark malty liquor. High-temperature firing (110°C) locks in aroma and reduces moisture to 3%.

Step 04 • Trade & Dispatch

Guwahati Auction & Global Shipping

Made tea is graded into Whole Leaf, Brokens, Fannings, and Dust, auctioned electronically at GTAC, and dispatched via Kolkata port to over 30 countries worldwide.
